Privacy Policy

BTC Peek for macOS · Effective October 18, 2026
HomeSupportPrivacy
Summary: the app collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no crash-reporting services. Nothing about you or your Mac is sent to the developer.

What the app does on the network

The app only makes outgoing HTTPS requests to download public market data (prices, funding rates, open interest and similar figures). It contacts only these hosts:

  • fapi.binance.com (Binance USD-M futures public market data)
  • api.bybit.com (Bybit public market data, used as a fallback)
  • api.exchange.coinbase.com (Coinbase Exchange public ticker, stats and candles)
  • api.coinbase.com (Coinbase public spot price, used as a fallback)

These requests contain no personal information, no account or device identifiers, and no API keys. Like any internet request, they reveal your IP address and a generic app identifier (BTCWidget/<version> (macOS)) to the provider being contacted. Those providers handle such requests under their own privacy policies. The developer does not operate any server and never receives this information.

What is stored on your Mac

The app stores only its preferences locally, in its own sandboxed preferences (UserDefaults): the panel's on-screen position. If you turn on Launch at login, macOS records that in System Settings > General > Login Items. A local diagnostic log of recent network requests and prices is kept inside the app's container and never leaves your Mac. Deleting the app removes all of this.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-up, login or accounts
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking of any kind, and no third-party SDKs
  • No access to your files, contacts, location, camera, microphone or other apps' data
  • No selling or sharing of data, because no data is collected

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
